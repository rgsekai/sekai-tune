Built from the ground up to ensure your music stays private, looks clean, and works perfectly.

Uninterrupted Playback

Background playback with no ads inside the app. Access your playlists, liked songs, and subscriptions instantly. Fast, lightweight, and supports local files.

Pro Audio Controls

EBU R128 loudness normalization, tempo & pitch controls, smooth crossfading, and deep integration with system equalizers and spatial audio.

Lyrics & Discovery

Live synced lyrics with AI translation and romanization. Built-in music recognition to find songs around you, plus detailed listening statistics.

Deep Customization

Material 3 design powered by album-art dynamic colors. Choose from up to 9 player styles and tailor gestures, animations, and layouts to fit your workflow.

Together Online & Social

Host or join shared listening rooms with friends in real time, build your Buddy List, and import your playlists from Spotify.
